# Ashes of the Sky — Game Design Document v0.2

## Project vision

Ashes of the Sky is an original story-driven aerial RPG about exploration, discovery, strategic aerial combat, and the evolving relationship between an explorer and a rare flying creature.

## World

The game takes place on **Elyndra**. An ancient civilization called **the Architects** built a network that combined technology, biology, and genetic engineering.

The Architects suddenly disappeared during an event known as **the Silence**. The truth behind their disappearance is connected to **the Dreamer**, a planet-scale artificial/biological intelligence.

## Protagonist

**Kael** is an explorer and ancient-technology scavenger, approximately 24 years old. Kael discovers a rare flying creature capable of interacting with Architect technology.

## Lunaris

**Lunaris** is not simply a mount; it is the game's second protagonist. Its evolution depends on player behaviour and progression.

The four evolutionary tendencies are:

- Predator
- Guardian
- Oracle
- Wraith

Hybrid evolution must eventually be possible. A Lunaris could, for example, express a profile of 70% Predator and 30% Oracle.

## Gameplay pillars

1. Exploration
2. Discovery
3. Strategic aerial combat
4. Relationship with and evolution of Lunaris

## World structure

The game should use large semi-open regions, not a seamless massive open world. This keeps authored discoveries meaningful and the production scope manageable.

The first prototype region is **the Valley of Ash**. Its major landmark is **the Spire**.

## Combat concept

Combat takes place while flying around enemies. Conceptually, an enemy has four positional quadrants:

- front;
- left;
- right;
- rear.

Each position may be dangerous, neutral, or vulnerable. Player actions consume a segmented energy/time system, initially conceived as three action segments:

| Action | Cost |
| --- | ---: |
| Basic attack | 1 |
| Movement | 1 |
| Defence | 1 |
| Special attack | 2 |
| Powerful attack | 3 |

This is a design direction only. The combat system must not be implemented during AOTS-001 or Sprint 001.

## Originality rule

Ashes of the Sky is an **original IP**. Panzer Dragoon Saga may be considered an emotional or high-level gameplay inspiration only.

Never use or reproduce Panzer Dragoon characters, names, lore, dragon designs, levels, symbols, music, dialogue, textures, models, code, story elements, or copyrighted Sega assets.

All new content must prioritize an original identity for Ashes of the Sky.
